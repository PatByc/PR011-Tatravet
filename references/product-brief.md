# Product brief

A Polish call dashboard for the Tatravet veterinary clinic in Zakopane. Staff need to see calls handled by their Vapi assistant, read transcripts, keep track of callbacks, and understand call volume and timing.

V1 includes a shared clinic login, overview, searchable call history, transcripts and optional provider summaries, callback status and notes, descriptive analytics, password change, and a copy-link action. Railway hosts the application, scheduled synchronization, and PostgreSQL. Public URL: https://liniavet.tatravet.pl.

Exclude recordings, provider costs, appointments/calendar management, individual staff accounts, public signup, multiple clinics, and inferred booking conversion. The voice assistant is developed separately; live acceptance waits for its configuration. The owner configures the custom domain and DNS.
